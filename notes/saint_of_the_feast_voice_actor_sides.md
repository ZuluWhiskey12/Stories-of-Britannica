# The Saint of the Feast Recording / Character Context Packet

## Character Direction

The Saint of the Feast is ancient, theatrical, intimate, and hungry. He should not sound like a simple villain. He sounds like a god that learned manners because manners make the knife go in easier.

The Saint's voice should feel beautiful and wrong. He can be warm, amused, patient, and almost gentle, but the warmth should always have teeth behind it. He speaks to Pela like she is already partly his, like the bargain was not made at the moment she accepted it, but at the moment she needed something badly enough to listen.

He is the Patron Saint of the Feast and the Lord of the Masquerade. The jester imagery matters: a smile that feels like a performance, a joke only he understands, a celebration no one survives unchanged. He does not always shout. Most of his strongest lines should feel quiet enough to be private, but powerful enough that the room obeys.

When he is amused, it should not sound harmless. When he is kind, it should make the listener suspicious. When he offers power, love, or belonging, it should sound like he understands exactly what Pela lacks and is willing to feed her with it.

For recording/reference, the actor should focus on the `SAINT` lines. The `NARRATOR`, `PELA`, `ODELYN`, `TAMA`, and other lines are included as cue/context lines so the scenes do not feel disconnected.

This packet covers the Saint's current scenes through Chapter 9, including the first idol scene, the oath, dream whispers, the Bochord warning, the Tama route pressure, and later Feast/hunger influence scenes.
