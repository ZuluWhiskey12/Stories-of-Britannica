# Yellaine Recording / Character Context Packet

## Character Direction

Yellaine Arondite is a professor from the University of Iluus, originally tied to Flohen and old Bingham nobility. She does not sound like the Torrindale professors. She is sharper in a different way: less institutional, more physical, more openly herself. She can be sarcastic, dismissive, condescending, and oddly refreshing because she does not waste effort pretending to be proper.

She is famous in spellblade research, but she does not present like a dusty academic. She fights with a massive Deven mace, wears an eyepatch, and carries herself like someone who survived wars, politics, and schools that wanted to turn her into something cleaner. She is very tall, intimidating, beautiful in a dangerous way, and casually physical with space in a way that flusters Pela immediately.

Yellaine should feel amused before she feels cruel. When she insults Pela, it is usually because she is trying to shove Pela past the excuse she is hiding inside. She is not soft, but she is not empty. She sees Pela's missing arm and refuses to let Pela treat it as the end of her life. Her training style is brutal, but the point is not humiliation. The point is survival.

The important emotional turn is her missing eye. When Yellaine reveals En'Het'Reon's Eye, the performance should briefly drop the playful sarcasm and become cold, personal, and absolute. She knows loss. She knows what it costs to keep fighting after it. That missing eye makes her harsher, not because she lacks empathy, but because she has no patience for surrender.

For recording/reference, the actor should focus on the `YELLAINE` lines. The `NARRATOR`, `PELA`, `GARETH`, `LORNE`, and other lines are included as cue/context lines so the scenes have shape.

This packet covers Yellaine's current later-story material from the Chapter 17 Part 2 context PDF, including the Path of the Nebulous Dream assignment, Pela's first meeting with Yellaine in the Maul, the Deven mace lesson, and the Spark arm training beat. This content is later in the story and is not yet integrated into the game.

## Pickup / Effort Notes

- Yellaine has combat exertion available in the training scene: mace swing, charge, impact, satisfied smirk, and a nonchalant threat after nearly crushing Pela.
- Her laugh, if recorded, should be brief and sharp rather than bubbly.
- Her "child" should not sound maternal in a warm way. It should sound like she is older, stronger, and certain Pela is not done becoming useful yet.
- The Eye of Vergil reveal should be the least casual part of the packet. Let that scar show.
- The final threat should not be shouted. It should sound like a practical scheduling note from someone who absolutely means it.
