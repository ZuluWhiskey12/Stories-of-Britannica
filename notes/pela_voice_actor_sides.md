# Pela Dianthus Recording / Character Context Packet

## Character Direction

Pela Dianthus is the heart of the story. She is brilliant, isolated, guarded, and hungry for proof that she is not a mistake. She is half human and half demon, and most rooms decide what that means before she speaks. The actor should carry that history in her pauses.

Pela is not timid because she has nothing to say. She is careful because almost every word she says can be turned into a weapon against her. Early Pela often sounds like she is measuring whether the room is safe enough for honesty. Later Pela starts speaking before the fear can stop her, and that is where the character grows.

Her intelligence matters. She understands magic better than many people around her, but she has been taught in ways that do not fit how she perceives magic. When she talks about spells, there should be curiosity, frustration, and relief. Spark magic should feel like being recognized by something she has been trying to reach for years.

Her emotional range is large:

- Public Pela: quiet, careful, tense, trying not to become a spectacle.
- Academic Pela: curious, precise, sometimes too eager once she forgets to be afraid.
- Friend Pela: awkward warmth, sincere gratitude, dry humor that still feels new in her mouth.
- Battle Pela: afraid, focused, increasingly dangerous, learning how to act while overwhelmed.
- Nerve-route Pela: sharp and confrontational, but not randomly cruel. She uses the edge because panic gave her a blade.
- Eldritch/Oath Pela: the sweetness in her voice can stop belonging to her. This should feel wrong, not simply evil.
- Unclaimed Pela: darker but clearer. She is not soft. She is becoming dangerous because she finally belongs to herself.

For recording/reference, the actor should focus on the `PELA` and `PELA THOUGHT` lines. The `NARRATOR`, `ARDENFELT`, `SAINT`, `GARETH`, `ODELYN`, `VAYNE`, `BELLADONNA`, `FORBIDDEN ONE`, `TAMA`, `ELIJAH`, `DAISY`, `PHILIP`, and other lines are included as cue/context lines so the scenes do not feel disconnected.

This packet covers Pela's current scenes through Chapter 9, including early Torrindale, Spark awakening, the Saint's oath, Gareth and Odelyn, Belladonna's lesson, the Bochord and Forbidden One, Lorne/Tama/Odelyn battles, the Mage-Card setup, Daisy, and Philip Gerkle.

## Pickup / Effort Notes

These are useful for future audio direction:

- Soft breath before saying something dangerous to a professor.
- A small startled laugh when Spark appears for the first time.
- A swallowed sob during public humiliation, especially if Bubble of Silence blocks her.
- Battle exertion: dodging, being hit, standing back up, and forcing Spark through pain.
- A frightened whisper when the Saint or Forbidden One is too close.
- A dry, defensive laugh with Gareth or Daisy when a room finally feels less cruel.
- A controlled, almost too-sweet delivery for Eldritch route lines.
- A steadier, lower delivery for Unclaimed route lines, as if Pela has stopped asking permission to exist.
