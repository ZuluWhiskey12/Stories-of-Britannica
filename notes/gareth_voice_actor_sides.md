# Gareth Voice Actor Sides

## Character Direction

Gareth is charming, quick, and socially practiced, but not hollow. His humor is armor, and sometimes that armor is too loud for the wound underneath it. He should sound like someone who knows how to win a room, but also like someone who has survived enough rooms to know when they are dangerous.

When he jokes, the actor should not make him careless. Gareth is usually watching the reaction his words create. When he is scared or embarrassed, he either talks too fast, hides behind pride, or tries to turn the feeling into a joke before anyone can use it against him.

The softer moments matter. Gareth should sound surprised by his own honesty, especially with Pela. He wants connection badly, but he is afraid of needing it.

For auditions, the actor should read the `GARETH` lines. The `NARRATOR`, `PELA`, `ODELYN`, `LORNE`, `ARDENFELT`, and other lines are included as cue/context lines so the scene does not feel like disconnected dialogue.

## Scene 1: First Meeting After Spark

Context: Pela has just drawn attention with Spark magic. Gareth catches up to her before the rumor can fully become a weapon.

Tone: Bright, curious, trying too hard to be charming. He is excited, but he is also testing whether Pela will let him close.

NARRATOR: Before Gareth reaches her, Pela hears the first rumor forming.

STUDENT: Did you see his coat?

STUDENT: Demon fire.

STUDENT: No, lightning.

STUDENT: No, worse. She smiled after.

NARRATOR: Pela had not smiled. She wonders how many versions of her already exist in mouths she will never control.

GARETH: Hey, wait!

NARRATOR: The copper-haired boy from the back rows jogs after her. He is too confident for someone who watched her nearly combust a professor.

GARETH: That was incredible.

GARETH: You're Pela, right?

PELA: Why have you been watching me?

GARETH: Fair.

NARRATOR: He holds up both hands. He stays at a respectful distance.

GARETH: But I am not here to mock you.

## Scene 2: The Warning

Context: Gareth's charm fades when he tells Pela that being noticed may have consequences outside the academy.

Tone: Serious, suddenly older. This is where the actor lets the mask slip.

NARRATOR: His name is Gareth. He is blunt enough to be irritating and sincere enough to be dangerous. He compliments her ears, her eyes, her hair, and somehow seems surprised when that does not make her trust him instantly.

NARRATOR: Then his smile fades.

GARETH: The Nhal of Athea may be watching this academy. They hunt what Athea's Sovereigne will not publicly touch.

PELA: Why do you care?

## Scene 3: His Secret

Context: Pela asks why Gareth cares. He answers with the part of himself that explains why he recognizes her danger.

Tone: Quiet, exposed, not melodramatic. He does not want pity.

GARETH: Because my mother is a naga.

NARRATOR: Pela blinks. He looks away first.

GARETH: They hate me too. Less loudly, maybe. But enough.

NARRATOR: For the first time, Pela hears loneliness in his confidence.

## Scene 4: Allies

Context: Gareth offers Pela a practical bond. He says allies, but the feeling underneath is almost-friendship.

Tone: Careful, vulnerable under restraint.

GARETH: We should stick together.

GARETH: Not friends, if that is too much. Allies.

NARRATOR: He offers his hand. Pela wants to believe him. Wanting is not the same as wisdom.

PELA: What are you afraid of?

NARRATOR: Gareth's grin twitches like it wants to become a joke and cannot find the strength.

GARETH: That I waited too long to stop pretending I was fine alone.

GARETH: And that you are smarter than me, so you will notice.

NARRATOR: Pela does notice. She also notices that he gave her the truth when mockery would have been easier.

## Scene 5: Jerk Route Banter

Context: Pela chooses a sharper tone and shuts down Gareth's attempt to charm her.

Tone: Playful, lightly wounded, amused that she hit the mark.

PELA: Compliments are not a strategy.

NARRATOR: Gareth blinks, then laughs once.

GARETH: I was hoping they were at least a distraction.

PELA: They are distracting in the way loose stairs are distracting.

NARRATOR: His smile turns genuine at the insult. That is either charming or a warning sign. Pela refuses to decide.

## Scene 6: Romance Route, Pela Notices

Context: Pela admits she noticed Gareth because she was looking. It catches both of them off guard.

Tone: Flustered softness under banter. Gareth tries to joke, then lets the silence live.

PELA: I noticed.

PELA: Because I was looking.

NARRATOR: Gareth's grin returns wrong at first, too quick and too bright. Then it softens into something that does not try to defend itself.

GARETH: Dangerous habit.

PELA: I have a few.

NARRATOR: For once, he does not immediately answer. Pela likes that more than she should.

## Scene 7: Duel With Odelyn

Context: Gareth fights Odelyn in front of the class. He performs confidence well, then says the wrong thing because pride is faster than wisdom.

Tone: Dramatic, proud, theatrical. Then sharp and careless when he insults Odelyn.

VAYNE: First match. Odelyn versus Gareth.

NARRATOR: Gareth smiles and waves at Pela as if being seen in a combat exam is normal. Odelyn summons a dark spear that crackles with heat. Gareth draws a jasmine-bright sword.

NARRATOR: Gareth moves first: fast, clean strikes meant to test rather than wound.

NARRATOR: Odelyn parries all of them. Her spearwork is a dance with no wasted motion. Wind slams Gareth backward, and the stage trembles under the impact.

NARRATOR: Gareth rises wrapped in white flame.

GARETH: The Phoenix of Gisborne rises twice!

NARRATOR: His second blade forms from fire. For several exchanges, he pushes Odelyn back. Sparks and wind shear across the wards. Then he smiles too sharply.

GARETH: With a borrowed power of your own, Cursed One.

## Scene 8: The Warning From Pela

Context: Gareth has stepped on a wound he does not understand. Pela sees Odelyn's spirits gathering and warns him.

Tone: Gareth has no line here, but this is key context for the next infirmary scenes.

NARRATOR: Odelyn's face goes cold. Black spirits gather around her spear. The air becomes heavy with old grief and violent obedience.

NARRATOR: Pela understands, suddenly, that Gareth has stepped on a wound he does not know how to survive.

PELA: Gareth, move!

NARRATOR: Pela's voice cracks across the stage. Gareth's eyes flick toward her, and that half-second is enough for fear to reach him before the spirits do. He raises his blades too late, but not blindly.

VAYNE: Enough.

NARRATOR: Odelyn is sent to the Dean. Gareth is sent to the infirmary. Pela is asked to follow.

## Scene 9: Infirmary Visit

Context: Pela visits Gareth after Odelyn hurts him badly. His usual grin is gone.

Tone: Bruised pride, embarrassed warmth. He is touched she came.

NARRATOR: Before Pela can face the Bochord, one worry keeps pulling her off course.

NARRATOR: Gareth.

NARRATOR: The infirmary corridor is quiet enough that her footsteps sound like interruptions. She pauses outside the door, hand half-raised, unsure whether concern will look foolish after only one strange day of almost-friendship.

NARRATOR: Gareth sits on a bed near the window, golden light softening the bruises along his jaw. The usual grin is gone. Without it, he looks less like a boy trying to win the room and more like someone realizing the room can hit back.

GARETH: Pela.

NARRATOR: He sounds surprised. Warmth follows the surprise before he can hide it.

GARETH: I didn't expect you.

PELA: I wanted to make sure you were okay.

GARETH: I'll live.

GARETH: Odelyn is... quite the opponent.

PELA: You held your own.

NARRATOR: His gaze softens at the compliment.

GARETH: That means a lot, coming from you.

NARRATOR: For a few seconds, silence does not feel empty. It feels like something choosing whether to become trust.

## Scene 10: Regret

Context: Pela confronts Gareth about calling Odelyn cursed. He does not defend it.

Tone: Shame. No charm left.

NARRATOR: Gareth winces, then tries to turn it into a laugh.

GARETH: Yes, well. I may have discovered a limit to my charm.

PELA: You called her cursed.

NARRATOR: The laugh dies properly this time.

GARETH: I know.

GARETH: I thought I was being clever. I was being cruel.

NARRATOR: That admission costs him something. Pela can see it.

## Scene 11: Together

Context: Pela leaves the infirmary. Gareth reaches out before either of them fully understands the gesture.

Tone: Gentle, scared to ask for closeness directly.

NARRATOR: Pela starts to leave. Gareth reaches out, his fingers brushing hers before either of them knows what to do with the contact.

GARETH: Don't be a stranger, okay? We're... we're in this together.

PELA: Together.

NARRATOR: The word follows her out of the infirmary, small and warm against the cold knot of the Bochord waiting ahead.

## Scene 12: Chapter Six Door Visit

Context: Gareth comes to Pela's room after the Lorne fight.

Tone: Concern hidden under casualness. He is worried about Pela, but he is also hiding something about why he missed the fight.

NARRATOR: Chapter 6: The Unseen.

NARRATOR: Someone knocks on Pela's door.

PELA: Who is it?

GARETH: It's me, Gareth. Can I come in?

NARRATOR: Pela opens the door without hiding anything because there is nothing ordinary about trying to look ordinary. Gareth stands outside with one hand lifted, as if he almost knocked again. He smiles when he sees her, but the smile has effort behind it.

PELA: Of course, come on in.

PELA: What's up?

GARETH: I just wanted to see how you were doing.

GARETH: I heard about the battle against Lorne.

PELA: Oh, yeah. I noticed that you weren't in class, what happened?

GARETH: Nothing, I was just feeling a little sick. It's no big deal.

PELA: Are you sure? I can't believe you'd miss that fight. It was incredible.

## Scene 13: Pressing The Sickness

Context: Pela notices Gareth's excuse does not fully hold.

Tone: Short, evasive, not ready to confess.

PELA: You missed the fight because you were sick?

NARRATOR: Gareth rubs the back of his neck.

GARETH: Something like that.

NARRATOR: It is not an answer. It is a door closing without a lock. Pela hears the invitation in that too: not now, but maybe someday.

## Scene 14: Lorne Victory

Context: Gareth asks how the fight went, already knowing there are consequences.

Tone: Proud of her first, then serious.

GARETH: Yeah, I heard. I'm sorry I missed it.

GARETH: How did it go?

PELA: I won.

PELA: It was tough, but I pulled through in the end.

GARETH: That's great! Congratulations.

GARETH: I knew you'd win. I never had a doubt in my mind.

PELA: Thanks.

NARRATOR: Pela's cheeks flush.

GARETH: I wanted to keep you updated on what's been going on at the Academy.

GARETH: The whole place has been buzzing with news about the fight.

GARETH: It's a big deal, you know. Having a duel in front of everyone where a previously unknown Mage comes in and wipes the floor with a transfer student from the College of Iluus at Brittanica City. People are talking, and they're not saying nice things about Lorne. He has a lot of raw power...

PELA: Oh, yeah? That's not surprising.

## Scene 15: The Fame Warning

Context: Gareth explains what Pela beating Lorne means politically.

Tone: Low, urgent, protective. He needs Pela to understand this.

GARETH: I know you don't realize this, but the reason that is significant is that he is supposed to be the strongest heir in his Royal House in terms of pure potential.

GARETH: He's a prodigy and a natural at combat magic. He has the highest potential in all of House Arcadia.

GARETH: But, you beat him. It's unheard of, no one has ever done that in their first battle. You've set a precedent, Pela. A really high bar, and people are going to want you to keep it up or destroy you to prove a point.

PELA: Great, no pressure.

GARETH: It'll be fine. You can handle it. You've got the skills, but do not seek that fame or else the Nhal will no doubt kill you. If you're not careful, you'll find yourself in a position where you can't handle that exposure. You have to be smart about this, Pela.

## Scene 16: Ward Bypass

Context: Pela asks how assassins could bypass Torrindale's defenses.

Tone: Too quick, practical, frighteningly informed. He has thought about this before.

PELA: How would assassins even get past Torrindale security?

GARETH: Invitations. Maintenance routes. Old service doors. People with names everyone trusts. Wards are strong until a person is allowed to stand on the wrong side of them.

NARRATOR: That is worse than a monster at the gate. Monsters can be barred. People can be welcomed in.

## Scene 17: Rumors About Gareth And Pela

Context: Gareth tells Pela that students are speculating about them.

Tone: Embarrassed, trying to be careful, stammering when romance is implied.

PELA: Thanks, Gareth. I'll be careful.

GARETH: Good, because the other thing is... there's been a lot of chatter about the two of us.

GARETH: It's mostly rumors, but people are speculating about us.

PELA: Speculating? About what?

GARETH: They think we're dating, or, at least, that we're going to start dating.

NARRATOR: Pela's mouth goes dry.

PELA: That is what they think?

GARETH: I know, but people are talking. They think that you and I have a connection, that we're close... I-mean-we-are-close-but-not-like-that.

## Scene 18: Keeping Their Heads Down

Context: Gareth explains why the rumors are dangerous beyond embarrassment.

Tone: Firm, protective, afraid of becoming a weak point someone can use against Pela.

PELA: We are.

PELA: But, we're not...you know...

GARETH: I know, I know.

GARETH: But, the other students don't know that, and they're making assumptions. We have to be careful. Any exposure is not necessarily good exposure. People will look for a weak link in your armor and exploit it if they can. Especially with me. People don't like me. They think I'm some kind of freak, and they're not shy about letting me know. I can take care of myself, but if they target you because of our friendship, that could be bad. We need to keep our heads down and stay under the radar as much as possible.

PELA: I'll try, but I can't promise anything.

GARETH: That's all I ask.

## Scene 19: Gareth's Reputation

Context: Pela asks what people say about him.

Tone: Bitter humor, hurt hidden under casual phrasing.

PELA: What do they say about you?

NARRATOR: Gareth smiles like the question struck a bruise.

GARETH: That I am strange. Useful when convenient. Dangerous when not. The usual academy poetry.

NARRATOR: Pela suddenly understands why he is warning her. He is not afraid of gossip. He knows what gossip becomes when powerful students need a target.

## Scene 20: The Book Question

Context: Gareth asks about the grimoire. Pela dodges. He notices, but lets her.

Tone: Gentle, suspicious but kind. He knows there is more, but he does not force it.

GARETH: Yeah, I'm fine. Don't worry about me.

GARETH: How are you doing? How's the book?

PELA: The book?

NARRATOR: Pela realizes she never told Gareth about it.

PELA: It's fine. Nothing to report.

GARETH: Okay. Well, if you ever need anything, you know where to find me.

GARETH: I'll see you later, okay?

PELA: Okay. See you.

## Scene 21: Almost Calling Him Back

Context: Gareth leaves. Pela almost asks him to stay, but chooses secrecy.

Tone: Gareth has no line here, but it is important emotional context for how his absence lands.

NARRATOR: Gareth turned and walked out of the room, leaving Pela alone with her thoughts.

NARRATOR: Pela almost calls him back.

NARRATOR: The words rise: stay, help, I think something is wrong with me. Then she imagines the grimoire, the Forbidden One, the Nhal, and Gareth caught in the middle of all of it.

NARRATOR: She lets the door close.

## Scene 22: Romance Worry

Context: Pela chooses the romance route while Gareth checks on her.

Tone: Sweet, embarrassed, mutual nervousness.

PELA: Are you all right?

NARRATOR: Gareth blinks at the concern. For a second, the practiced ease falls away and he looks like someone who has been carrying a secret through crowded halls. Then he nods too quickly.

GARETH: I am. I just wanted to check on you.

PELA: I like when you worry about me.

PELA: That sounded less embarrassing in my head.

GARETH: It did not sound embarrassing from here.

NARRATOR: Then he looks embarrassed enough for both of them. The room becomes quiet in a way that does not feel empty.

## Scene 23: Odelyn Route Branch

Context: If Pela has leaned closer to Odelyn, Gareth steps aside so Odelyn can talk to her.

Tone: A little wounded, but honest and mature. He is not abandoning Pela. He is admitting Odelyn is better for this conversation.

GARETH: That's great! Congratulations.

GARETH: I knew you'd win. I never had a doubt in my mind.

NARRATOR: The grin holds for half a second too long. Then his eyes move toward the door.

GARETH: Actually.

GARETH: Someone else should talk to you about what that win means.

PELA: Gareth?

GARETH: I'm not leaving because I want to.

GARETH: I'm leaving because she has sharper teeth for this conversation than I do.

PELA: Are you avoiding something?

GARETH: A lot.

NARRATOR: The honesty is so quick that Pela almost misses it.

GARETH: But not you. Not this.

NARRATOR: He steps aside before the sentence can become anything softer.

ODELYN: Gareth told you about Lorne?

PELA: Not yet.

ODELYN: Good. Then I can do it worse.

## Scene 24: Unclaimed Rumor Branch

Context: Pela refuses to belong to the rumors around them.

Tone: Gareth does not have much dialogue here, but his reaction is important: he expected fear and found resolve.

PELA: We can be careful.

PELA: But I will not make myself smaller because someone else is bored enough to name me.

NARRATOR: Gareth looks at her like he expected fear and found a blade instead.

PELA: I do not belong to their rumor.

PELA: And neither do you.

NARRATOR: For a moment, the room feels less like hiding. It feels like choosing where to stand.

## Scene 25: Weekend Spellblade Advice

Context: Pela visits Gareth in his dorm room before Spellblade.

Tone: Casual warmth, mock offense, then genuine advice.

GARETH: Hey, Pela.

GARETH: What brings you here?

PELA: I know you might not be the best person to ask, but what do you think I should do to prepare for Spellblade tomorrow?

NARRATOR: Pela genuinely asks while taking a friendly jab at Gareth for losing so bad to Odelyn. She is laughing on the inside and hopes he will take that in good humor.

GARETH: I'm not the right person to ask? Ouch.

GARETH: That was just a minor setback.

NARRATOR: He says it in an attempt to recover his pride.

GARETH: But, you're right. From what I know of Professor Vayne, she likes to push her students quite a bit.

NARRATOR: Gareth thinks for a moment, as if deciding how comforting honesty is supposed to be.

GARETH: So if she thinks you're weak, she will place you against a strong opponent. If she thinks you are strong, she will make you face a very difficult opponent. Either way, she will make you fight someone who is a challenge.

NARRATOR: Pela winces in pain at that realization.

## Scene 26: Lorne Advice Before Spellblade

Context: Gareth gives Pela useful information before her battle with Lorne.

Tone: Practical, concerned, trying to make danger sound manageable.

GARETH: You will have to show her that you are strong, and capable of facing anyone who she throws your way. Think of it as a compliment in a weird way.

PELA: There really isn't anything else you could let me know?

NARRATOR: Gareth scratches his neck.

GARETH: Well...

GARETH: I've heard he is from Iluus and uses impact magic, so as long as you were paying attention in Professor Belladonna's class it shouldn't be anything you haven't seen before.

NARRATOR: Pela visibly winces at that, and Gareth continues.

GARETH: But, you have never seen impact magic used offensively at this level, so just be careful.

PELA: Thanks, Gareth.

## Scene 27: Cart To The Mage-Card Exam

Context: Ardenfelt meets Pela, Lorne, Odelyn, and Gareth at the cart.

Tone: Respectful, uneasy. Gareth knows not to joke with Ardenfelt.

NARRATOR: The next day, Pela, Lorne, Odelyn, and Gareth arrived at the cart that would take them toward the arena. The morning looked too ordinary for something that could decide whether they were allowed to keep existing.

ARDENFELT: So, you've made it.

ARDENFELT: But where are the other two?

GARETH: I apologize, Professor.

GARETH: We have no idea who the other two are.

ARDENFELT: No matter. Maybe they heard the Dean's speech and no longer have the nerve. Either way, get in the cart or suffer the humiliation of excommunication.

## Scene 28: Brittanica City

Context: Gareth and Pela see the capital city.

Tone: Awed, solemn, without jokes. This is one of the rare places where Gareth just lets himself feel something.

NARRATOR: Pela, Odelyn, Gareth, and Lorne stood at the massive gates of Brittanica City. The silver gates were adorned with intricate designs and the symbol of the Empire. The city rose beyond them with walls, murals, arches, vines, and flowers. The Seat of Magical Power was not an exaggeration.

PELA: So, this is the Capital of the Empire.

PELA: It's so beautiful.

GARETH: Yes.

GARETH: Yes it is.

PELA: It is beautiful.

NARRATOR: Pela shares the awe with Gareth because he is the only one not trying to own the view.

GARETH: It makes me feel like I should stand up straighter.

NARRATOR: Pela almost laughs. Then she realizes she is doing it too.

## Scene 29: The Barrier Stage

Context: The team tries to make sense of the next stage of the exam. Lorne tries to brute-force it.

Tone: Dry humor under pressure.

PELA: Wait.

PELA: The second stage? What do you think the first stage was?

GARETH: Who knows.

ODELYN: Maybe the written test.

LORNE: Who cares.

NARRATOR: Lorne walks forward. The barrier bounces him backward into the ground.

GARETH: We are Brittanican mages. We can handle this.

NARRATOR: He believed they needed to apply Ardenfelt's fundamental classes and channel the proper amount of mana.

PELA: Lorne, I can't believe you didn't learn from when we fought. This is just another Impact Shield.

LORNE: Then I'll just break it.

LORNE: Greater Dark Impact: Overwhelming Destruction.

NARRATOR: The barrier shattered, then reformed instantly.

LORNE: Damnit, this barrier is annoying.

GARETH: It's a barrier. Of course it's going to be annoying.

PELA: That was almost elegant.

GARETH: Almost?

PELA: Do not get greedy.

NARRATOR: Gareth smiles despite the barrier, and for a second the exam feels less like an execution machine and more like something they might survive by being themselves.

## Scene 30: Lorne Down

Context: Raithgar drops Lorne after overpowering him.

Tone: Alarmed, genuine, not joking.

NARRATOR: Raithgar dropped Lorne.

GARETH: Is he dead?

KNIGHT: Not dead.

NARRATOR: Kyrehae knelt beside Lorne and slapped him hard enough to draw more blood and wake him. Lorne coughed weakly.

## Scene 31: Tournament Names

Context: Lorne explains the points system and names the strongest teams.

Tone: Curious, trying to gather useful information.

LORNE: It's points based on the spells we cast and our performance against our opponent.

LORNE: There are only a few slots for the finals. The better you do, the better your chance.

GARETH: Who are the best mages in Brittanica?

LORNE: Kyrehae, Prince Reglio, Raithgar, and Prince Nalto's team are all from the Academy of Cinn.

## Scene 32: Reglio

Context: Pela sees Reglio's power and beauty.

Tone: Awed, almost reverent.

PELA: His magical energy is incredible.

NARRATOR: She could appreciate his power. She was also focused on his beauty. Almost feminine.

GARETH: He looks like an angel.

LORNE: If he looks like an angel, then his sister looks like a demon.

## Scene 33: Shared Fear

Context: Romance/trust branch during the exam. Pela admits fear without covering it with a joke.

Tone: Honest fear, quiet steadiness. Gareth matches her instead of trying to fix it.

PELA: I do not like how small he makes the road feel.

NARRATOR: Gareth looks at her, then at Reglio's back.

GARETH: Me neither.

NARRATOR: It does not comfort her. It steadies her, which is rarer.

## Optional Audition Notes

Recommended sustained audition scenes:

1. Scene 1 through Scene 4 tests charm, warning, and vulnerability.
2. Scene 9 through Scene 11 tests wounded pride and regret.
3. Scene 14 through Scene 18 tests protective urgency and awkward romance pressure.
4. Scene 25 through Scene 26 tests lighter Gareth and practical advice.
5. Scene 28 through Scene 33 tests group dynamic, humor under danger, awe, and fear.

Gareth should never sound like he is only comic relief. Even when he is funny, the joke usually has a purpose: keep control of the room, lower the temperature, hide embarrassment, or keep fear from becoming visible.
