# Odelyn Recording / Character Context Packet

## Character Direction

Odelyn is blunt, disciplined, cursed, and protective in a way that often sounds like a warning before it sounds like care. She speaks like dangerous information is ordinary because she has had to live around danger long enough to stop treating it like a surprise.

She is not emotionless. She is someone who keeps emotion locked behind her teeth until it becomes useful. When she is soft, the softness should feel rare and a little unwilling, like it escaped before she could decide whether it was safe. When she is angry, it is usually because fear got there first.

Her curse matters. People call her cursed like that explains her, but it does not. The actor should play her as someone who knows what people assume about her before they say it. She has old grief, old training, and a sharp survival instinct. She can sound severe because she is trying to keep someone alive.

For recording/reference, the actor should focus on the `ODELYN` lines. The `NARRATOR`, `PELA`, `GARETH`, `LORNE`, `VAYNE`, `ELIJAH`, `LYSANDER`, and other lines are included as cue/context lines so the scene has shape.

This packet covers Odelyn's current scenes through Chapter 9, including common scenes, romance/unclaimed/jerk branches, and her battle with Pela in The Lost.
