# The Forbidden One Recording / Character Context Packet

## Character Direction

The Forbidden One is a demon, a teacher, a prisoner, and a threat that knows how to sound like an answer. Their danger is not only that they lie. Their danger is that they often tell the truth in the exact shape Pela needs to hear.

The voice should feel calm before it feels cruel. The Forbidden One does not need to shout to control a room. They enjoy precision. They enjoy watching Pela learn. They enjoy being needed. When they are amused, it should feel old and patient, not playful in a harmless way. When they threaten, it should feel like the room has already agreed with them.

They are not only a monster in the dark. They are a brilliant instructor with a leash hidden inside every lesson. Their best moments should sound useful enough that Pela hating them becomes complicated.

For recording/reference, the actor should focus on the `FORBIDDEN ONE` lines. The `NARRATOR`, `PELA`, `ODELYN`, and other lines are included as cue/context lines so the scene does not feel like disconnected dialogue.

This packet covers The Forbidden One's current scenes through Chapter 9, including the Bochord bargain, the first grimoire lesson, battle whispers, weekend instruction, Chapter 7 corruption, and later presence/influence.

## Scene 1: The Figure In The Room Of Stories

Context: Pela is inside the Bochord and encounters a figure who offers her The Disaster of Seriphina. At this point, the Forbidden One is not fully named. They present themself like a solution.

Tone: Ancient, welcoming, too composed. The kindness should feel deliberate.

NARRATOR: Pela's hand hovers over The Disaster of Seriphina.

NARRATOR: The book feels warm.

NARRATOR: Pela takes the book, and the Bochord shifts around her until the shelves, floors, and room stop behaving like architecture.

FORBIDDEN ONE: Hello, child.

NARRATOR: The figure offers history, order, peace, secrets, a future where old wrongs can be corrected by someone brave enough to read what was buried.

NARRATOR: He holds out The Disaster of Seriphina.

FORBIDDEN ONE: You can understand the true power of the gods.

NARRATOR: Pela's soul tightens around the Saint's oath.

PELA: I am not ready.

FORBIDDEN ONE: Then why take the book?

NARRATOR: Pela has no clean answer. Curiosity. Fear. Hunger. The awful hope that forbidden knowledge might be kinder than public education.

PELA: I am not ready.

FORBIDDEN ONE: You could be.

## Scene 2: Freedom As Bait

Context: Pela asks whether the figure can break the Saint of the Feast's hold. The Forbidden One offers freedom, but frames need as more important than trust.

Tone: Intimate, patient, tempting. The last line should land like a hook, not a threat.

PELA: Can you break the Saint's hold?

FORBIDDEN ONE: What if I taught you to break the Saint's hold?

NARRATOR: The possibility is intoxicating.

NARRATOR: Freedom from the Feast. Freedom from the mark that vanished but did not leave. Freedom from every bargain made while terrified.

PELA: I can't trust you.

FORBIDDEN ONE: No.

FORBIDDEN ONE: But you can need me.

## Scene 3: The Forbidden Revealed

Context: The figure reveals themself after using Odelyn's voice as bait. This is where "helpful" becomes openly coercive.

Tone: Smooth, cold, theatrical only because the space belongs to them. The threat should feel absolute.

NARRATOR: Odelyn's voice calls from the dark.

NARRATOR: Pela turns, and Odelyn melts into the floor like candle wax.

NARRATOR: The figure rises from the puddle she leaves behind.

FORBIDDEN ONE: I am trapped within the Bochord. My enemies call me The Forbidden. Take me to Torrindale Academy, and we can learn together.

NARRATOR: Pela backs away.

FORBIDDEN ONE: Refuse, and I keep you in the Room of Stories forever. I will make new paths in your mind until your mind is mine.

PELA: If I take you, you promise to teach me?

FORBIDDEN ONE: I would assure it. I would assure you. I would not lie.

NARRATOR: There is always a catch. Pela knows that now. The Forbidden One wants freedom after she becomes a proper mage.

## Scene 4: Terms

Context: Pela tries to demand clearer terms before swearing. The Forbidden One respects the teeth in the question, but that does not make them safe.

Tone: First pause should be genuinely interested. Then amused approval.

PELA: Define proper mage.

NARRATOR: For the first time, the Forbidden One pauses.

FORBIDDEN ONE: One who can shape magic without begging a book to feel for her.

PELA: Then you teach. You do not command.

NARRATOR: The smile returns.

FORBIDDEN ONE: A student with teeth. Very well.

NARRATOR: The Forbidden One holds out The Disaster of Seriphina.

FORBIDDEN ONE: Do you swear that you will free me, and in exchange I will teach you magic and give you knowledge others would kill for?

PELA: I swear.

NARRATOR: The world shatters like glass.

## Scene 5: The Jerk Route Pushback

Context: Pela insults the Forbidden One's theatrics. The Forbidden One adjusts immediately and proves that the drama was optional.

Tone: Amused, then cruelly adaptable. "Terror instead" should be almost polite.

PELA: You are losing the argument by being dramatic.

NARRATOR: The Forbidden One's smile does not move.

FORBIDDEN ONE: Am I?

PELA: A little.

FORBIDDEN ONE: Then I will try terror instead.

NARRATOR: The room presses against Pela's mind hard enough to make her knees tremble. She hates that her mouth still wants to answer.

## Scene 7: Emotional Infection

Context: Pela thinks she is using the grimoire. The Forbidden One corrects her by manipulating her emotional state and then calling it a lesson.

Tone: Amused, clinical, frighteningly calm. The actor should make the demonstration feel worse because it is so clean.

FORBIDDEN ONE: Use the book?

NARRATOR: The Forbidden One sounds amused.

FORBIDDEN ONE: The book is using you. It has infected your emotional state.

NARRATOR: The rage drains away.

NARRATOR: Pela is calm. Too calm. The sudden absence of fear feels like losing a limb she had been balancing on.

FORBIDDEN ONE: There.

FORBIDDEN ONE: An example.

PELA: Do not touch my feelings without permission.

NARRATOR: The Forbidden One is silent long enough for the room to creak.

FORBIDDEN ONE: Good. Control begins with noticing the hand on the leash.

## Scene 8: Awe

Context: The Forbidden One teaches Pela to shape demon-kind magic through powerful emotion. This is one of their clearest teacher scenes.

Tone: Precise, instructional, almost beautiful. The voice should make the lesson feel irresistible.

NARRATOR: The Forbidden One asks Pela to remember Odelyn's spear.

FORBIDDEN ONE: Not envy.

FORBIDDEN ONE: Awe.

FORBIDDEN ONE: Demon-kind shape magic through powerful emotion. Human incantations ask the world to move. Demon-kind make the world feel why it must.

NARRATOR: Pela closes her eyes and remembers the spear's heat, its worn darkness, its impossible presence.

NARRATOR: A faint outline appears before Pela.

NARRATOR: Barely visible. Barely real. It is almost nothing.

FORBIDDEN ONE: Good.

PELA: That is generous.

FORBIDDEN ONE: It is honest.

## Scene 9: The Spear

Context: Pela successfully forms her first Spark spear with the grimoire's help. The Forbidden One praises her, then immediately takes it away.

Tone: Genuine pride with possessive control underneath. The last half should reveal that approval was never freedom.

NARRATOR: The grimoire opens beneath her hands.

NARRATOR: It does not give her power. It gives her too much feeling. Awe becomes obsession. Every detail of Odelyn's spear becomes beautiful enough to hurt.

NARRATOR: The room fills with the smell of burning metal.

NARRATOR: A spear forms in the air.

NARRATOR: Not Odelyn's. Pela's.

NARRATOR: Pale blue metal. Silver-white tip. Crimson handle. A pink sash at the end. Electricity hums through it like a held note.

PELA: Wow.

FORBIDDEN ONE: Impressive.

FORBIDDEN ONE: I am very pleased. However, I will be taking that from you.

NARRATOR: The spear disintegrates.

PELA: That's not fair.

FORBIDDEN ONE: Now that you know how it feels, you cannot have the book again until you master the magic and the emotions that go with it.

PELA: That's not fair.

FORBIDDEN ONE: Neither is talent without control.

PELA: You're a jerk.

FORBIDDEN ONE: Perhaps. I am also the best teacher you will ever have.

Context: Pela opens the grimoire again before Spellblade class. She is careful because she knows the Forbidden One can help her, and that is exactly what makes the situation dangerous.

Tone: Warm enough to unsettle. The Forbidden One should sound pleased that Pela returned.

NARRATOR: The room goes dark and it greets her again.

FORBIDDEN ONE: Pela, you've come for my guidance once again.

## Scene 14: Weekend Lesson, Beyond Fusion

Context: The Forbidden One explains that demons can fuse emotions with spells. This is educational, but the example is violent and revealing.

Tone: Professorial and frightening. Do not make the violence sound like a joke. Make it sound like an answer they know Pela will remember.

FORBIDDEN ONE: Your professors have already told you about Fusion, but what if I told you that you can fuse more than just spells together?

PELA: What do you mean?

FORBIDDEN ONE: Demons can use their emotions to layer their magical abilities as well as spells. You, being a half-demon, can do this as well.

PELA: I don't quite understand.

FORBIDDEN ONE: It's simple. You fuse a spell and your emotions. Let me give you an example to make things easier for you to understand.

FORBIDDEN ONE: Let's say you want to cast Spark. When you do so, you can also layer on an emotion to the spell, and it will enhance the spell.

NARRATOR: The Forbidden One lets the dark sit on the word emotion a little too long.

FORBIDDEN ONE: If you cast Spark and are filled with pure hatred, your Spark can become an explosion of pure electric energy, which will incinerate anything it touches.

PELA: Wow.

NARRATOR: That is insane, she thought, her mind amazed.

NARRATOR: With that, the Forbidden One and Pela take that newfound knowledge and both equally gain from that conversation.

NARRATOR: They start practicing and honing Pela's abilities.

NARRATOR: The lesson works. That is the part Pela hates most.

## Scene 15: Destiny Poison

Context: After the Dean's warning and Elijah's threat, Pela reads the book while frightened. The Forbidden One presses exactly where fear has made room.

Tone: Whispered grandeur, poisonous comfort. The actor should make the words feel like relief first and corruption second.

NARRATOR: Later that night, Pela sat in her room, reading the book.

NARRATOR: Pela lets the chosen feeling unfold.

NARRATOR: It is warm in the way fever is warm. Wrong, but comforting. Dangerous, but shaped exactly like relief.

FORBIDDEN ONE: You are strong, Pela. You are a chosen one, destined for greatness. I will help you achieve your destiny.

NARRATOR: Pela spirals as she reads.

PELA: There is a reason the book chose me. I'm special, I'm the chosen one. The book chose me, not some random student. It's my destiny. The Nhal, Athea's Sovereigne, the Royal Families, they'll all bow to my power. None of them will stop me. Not anymore. I won't let them. They'll tremble at my feet, begging for mercy.

NARRATOR: A shadowy gray smoke starts manifesting in the corner of Pela's room.

NARRATOR: The whisper knows where to press.

NARRATOR: Chosen. Strong. Destined. Pela should hate the words for how easily they find her wounds. Instead, she leans closer before she remembers to be afraid.

## Scene 16: The God Promise

Context: The Forbidden One's voice joins with another sinister presence. The offer becomes total: power, knowledge, wealth, love.

Tone: Expansive and hypnotic. It should sound like the Forbidden One is building a throne in Pela's mind.

FORBIDDEN ONE: Yes, that is it. That is the path, the destiny. You will become a god. You will have all the power, the knowledge, the wealth, the love. All of it is yours.

ODELYN: What have you done, Pela? Do not let this power corrupt you. It is not about the power itself, it is about the responsibility and the potential. If you misuse this power, it will consume you and destroy everything around you.

NARRATOR: Odelyn appears from the shadowy darkness and rushes over to Pela and closes her book.

PELA: No, no. You're wrong. I'm destined to use this power, to change the world. I can't ignore my fate.

NARRATOR: Pela closes a hand over the book, not opening it, not letting it go.

ODELYN: That is how it keeps you. One reasonable inch at a time.

## Scene 17: After Odelyn's Lesson

Context: During Odelyn's attempt to pull Pela back from the book, the Forbidden One speaks softly. The line sounds encouraging, but it is dangerous because it echoes the same identity wound.

Tone: Soft, almost merciful, but ambiguous. It can be read as comfort, manipulation, or both.

NARRATOR: Pela's heart feels heavy. She knows Odelyn is right to an extent.

NARRATOR: But she cannot just give up on becoming a great mage. She has to become stronger, or the Nhal will hunt her down. And if she is not careful, the Ashcrofts will have her executed.

NARRATOR: The thought crosses her mind, and she realizes that was the book speaking.

FORBIDDEN ONE: It's not too late. You can still change, Pela. You can still be more than a demon.

ODELYN: You're not a monster, Pela. I can tell that much. You just need to find the courage, the will, to push past your emotions and become a great mage.

## Scene 18: Presence Without Speech

Context: After Odelyn's battle, the Forbidden One is not always speaking, but the game frames the grimoire as possessive pressure. This helps the actor understand how their earlier lines should echo in later scenes.

Tone Note: The Forbidden One's silence should feel like a book left open in the room. Not gone. Waiting.

NARRATOR: The lesson with Odelyn does not leave when Odelyn does.

NARRATOR: Pela sits in the quiet after The Lost and feels the shape of every place she is still weak.

NARRATOR: The Forbidden One has been important. Too important. Prolonged exposure to that grimoire is not education anymore. It is a ramp. It is a hand on her back. It is possessive power pretending to be guidance.

NARRATOR: Pela decides one thing before leaving the office.

NARRATOR: She will not pass as Ardenfelt's correction. She will not pass as the Saint's appetite. She will not pass as the Forbidden One's crony.

NARRATOR: If she survives, she will survive as Pela Dianthus, and she does not know whether that is bravery or stubbornness.
