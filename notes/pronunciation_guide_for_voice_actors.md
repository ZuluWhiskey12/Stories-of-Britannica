# Stories of Britannica Pronunciation Guide for Voice Actors

## How To Use This Guide

This is a suggested canon pronunciation guide for the current build. It focuses on story-specific names, places, factions, magic terms, titles, and words that may not be obvious from spelling alone.

Format:

- **Term** - `pronunciation` - quick context.

Stress is shown in all caps. For example, `tor-in-DAYL` means the final syllable is stressed.

If a future script or actor direction changes a pronunciation, update this file first so every VA stays consistent.

## Core Title / Series Terms

- **Demons of an Idol Dawn** - `DEE-munz uv an EYE-dol dawn` - the main title. Dark fantasy, formal, ominous.
- **Stories of Britannica** - `STOR-eez uv brih-TAN-ih-kuh` - the game/story umbrella title.
- **Stories of Brittanica** - `STOR-eez uv brih-TAN-ih-kuh` - alternate project spelling used in some notes. Pronounce the same way.
- **Pela Dianthus: A Heart Hungers** - `PEH-lah dye-AN-thus: uh hart HUN-gurz` - subtitle/arc title. The hunger should feel emotional and supernatural.
- **Brittanica** - `brih-TAN-ih-kuh` - the empire/country. Do not pronounce like real-world "Britannica" with a polished documentary feel. This is a living empire with cruelty under the marble.
- **Brittanica Ultima** - `brih-TAN-ih-kuh UHL-tih-mah` - Pela's home city/region. More personal and nostalgic when Pela thinks of it.

- **Belladonna** - `bel-uh-DON-uh` - do not over-soften it. He is precise and frighteningly practical.
- **Professor Vayne** - `VAYN` - sharp, confident, martial. The name lands like a blade.

## Common Performance Notes

- **Professor / formal address:** Use formal surname pronunciation. Authority figures often say "Miss Dianthus" with pressure.
- **Insults:** Words like "monster," "half-demon," and "Cursed One" should land differently depending on speaker. Lorne weaponizes them. Odelyn understands the wound behind them. Pela absorbs them before deciding whether to answer.
- **Divine/demonic titles:** The Saint of the Feast should feel seductive and predatory. The Forbidden One should feel calmer, more intimate, and more intellectually dangerous.
- **Royal names:** Reglio, Kyrehae, Raithgar, Nalto, and Ancala should carry social weight. Characters who know them should sound like they know the danger attached.
- **Academy terms:** Spell names and techniques should be clear and consistent, especially for battle recording.
