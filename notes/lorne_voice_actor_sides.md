# Lorne Recording / Character Context Packet

## Character Direction

Lorne is arrogant, sharp, and cruel in the way someone becomes when they think status is the only proof that pain meant something. He is not merely a bully. He is a royal-blooded student who has learned to treat weakness like a disease because the world he comes from punishes it publicly.

He should sound polished, entitled, and mean on purpose. When Lorne insults someone, he is trying to put the room back into an order where he is above them. He is House Arcadia's little miracle, a transfer from Iluus, and a self-proclaimed Dark Mage. He wants his name, robe, bloodline, and Impact magic to mean the fight is already decided.

The actor should not play him as constantly screaming. Lorne is most dangerous when he is controlled and condescending. His anger should sharpen quickly when he is embarrassed. When he loses control, it is because Pela, Odelyn, or someone higher in the royal structure has proven that his confidence is not actually safety.

There is also a wound under him. Lorne understands excommunication. He knows what Brittanican magical power does to people who fail. He is still cruel, but his cruelty has an education behind it. When Reglio, Kyrehae, Raithgar, or exile come up, Lorne should sound different: less theatrical, more aware that there are monsters in the system bigger than him.

For recording/reference, the actor should focus on the `LORNE` lines. The `NARRATOR`, `PELA`, `ODELYN`, `GARETH`, `VAYNE`, `KYREHAE`, and `RAITHGAR` lines are included as cue/context lines so the scene has shape.

This packet covers Lorne's current scenes through Chapter 9, including the first Spellblade induction duel, his post-duel humiliation, the Mage-Card Exam setup, Brittanica City, the royal siblings, and the first hints that Lorne fears power above his own.
