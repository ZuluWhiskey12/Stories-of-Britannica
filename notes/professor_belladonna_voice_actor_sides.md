# Professor Belladonna Recording / Character Context Packet

## Character Direction

Professor Belladonna is severe, exacting, and frighteningly practical. He does not try to make students like him. In fact, he seems to accept being disliked as part of the work. He teaches as if every classroom mistake might one day become a corpse, and that belief makes his cruelty feel purposeful instead of careless.

He should not sound like a cartoon villain. Belladonna is controlled. Even when he threatens Pela with expulsion, he is not losing his temper. He is applying pressure. He uses fear as a tool and expects students to become sharper under it. That does not make him kind, but it does make him dangerous in a more grounded way.

His exhaustion matters. In this section, he arrives with stubble and wrinkled clothes, looking like someone who has stopped caring whether he frightens people. The actor should carry tiredness under the authority. Belladonna sounds like a professor who has seen what happens outside protected rooms, and he has no patience for gentle failure.

His voice should be precise, dry, and cutting. He can be funny in a deadpan way, especially when he says he is "not irresponsible" and "simply disliked." His praise should feel rare, not warm. When he says Pela did well, it should still sound like an evaluation.

For recording/reference, the actor should focus on the `BELLADONNA` lines. The `NARRATOR`, `PELA`, `ERIKA`, `STUDENT`, and other lines are included as cue/context lines so the classroom pressure stays intact.

This packet covers Professor Belladonna's current scenes through the expanded Negative Impact lesson, including Pela arriving late, Negative Bolt, Negative Burst, the expulsion threat, branch reactions to Pela's shield, Erika's question, and the after-class explanation.
